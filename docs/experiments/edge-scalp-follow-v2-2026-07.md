# Edge + Pulse Scalp Follow v2 replay — conclusions

## Scope

- Edge used the actual 5-minute BUY stream generated by the prior Edge minute experiment.
- Pulse used the paper-only Scalp Follow v2 state machine with a 0.30% runtime half-width, ATR-aware discrete re-centering, frozen entry/target/stop brackets, loss cooldown, and end-of-day liquidation.
- Test period: July 7–17, 2026, nine sessions held out from Pulse parameter selection.
- Universe: the same 20 symbols and one-minute bars used in the earlier experiment.
- Fixed $500 buying power per ticker; $10,000 starting portfolio; 10-basis-point total round-trip cost.
- Edge entry windows tested: 15, 30 and 60 minutes after an actual selected BUY signal.
- Edge maximum-entry prices were enforced.
- Edge initial-stop tightening and experimental signal-expiry supervision were tested separately.

## Best role for Edge in this sample

The 30-minute strict authorization gate produced the best balance among the tested Edge windows:

- 40 approved entries instead of 556 autonomous Pulse trades.
- Drawdown fell from 7.39% to 1.46%.
- Win rate rose from 60.25% to 75.00%.
- Profit factor rose from 1.435 to 2.272.
- The ordinary-stock group changed from -$93.32 to +$18.37.
- Total P&L fell from +$923.66 to +$195.12 because Edge rejected most Pulse opportunities.

This supports using Edge as a selective **entry authorization and sizing layer**, not as a continuous replacement for Pulse's scalp state machine.

## Why the 30-minute gate won and lost

| Exit reason | Trades | Net P&L | Average P&L |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| End of day | 1 | -$2.40 | -$2.40 |
| Hard stop | 9 | -$150.94 | -$16.77 |
| Profit target | 30 | $348.46 | $11.62 |

The gate won because 30 target exits generated $348.46, more than offsetting nine hard stops and one end-of-day exit. The result remained penny-heavy, but the ordinary bucket was positive rather than negative.

Top contributors were BIYA $70.01, BATL $66.11, CJMB $27.24, SLND $13.39, and MSFT $8.36. The weakest were MU -$0.31, AMAT -$2.40, and PLTR -$5.28.

## Edge supervisory experiments that did not help

1. **Atomic initial-stop tightening had no effect.** None of Edge's actual initial stops was tighter than Pulse's frozen 1.5R stop for the approved entries, so the result was identical to the plain 30-minute gate.
2. **Signal-expiry reductions reduced profit.** Four break-even stop updates and six 50% loss reductions lowered P&L from $195.12 to $155.23 and increased drawdown. This policy should not be promoted from this sample.
3. **Advisory-only Edge changed little.** Allowing Pulse to enter autonomously preserved 560 trades and nearly reproduced Pulse-only expectancy; Edge touched too few entries to change the ordinary-stock problem.

## Interpretation limits

- Edge's selected signal stream was generated across the complete month, while the Pulse policy parameters used a 10-session/9-session split. This is stronger than a same-bar synthetic overlay but is not a fully independent Edge walk-forward validation.
- The raw communication CSV contains repeated per-minute rejected entry touches. Those rows are diagnostics, not unique trading decisions.
- Penny symbols were selected after observing monthly breakouts, so their contribution remains selection-biased.
- One-minute bars cannot establish queue position, quote latency, partial fills, or the order of target and stop touches inside a candle.
- No live settings or live orders were changed.

## SPY/QQQ exact-data validation

A separate runner and GitHub Actions workflow now:

1. downloads one-minute SPY and QQQ bars and produces stop-first/target-first bounds;
2. attempts authenticated Alpaca SIP historical quotes/trades;
3. falls back to authenticated IEX quotes/trades if SIP entitlement is unavailable;
4. replays entry at the ask and exits at the bid;
5. records quote/trade counts and median spread;
6. explicitly reports `exact_provider=unavailable` rather than claiming tick accuracy when credentials or entitlement are absent.

The job completed with 7,409 one-minute bars and 19 sessions for each ETF, but no authenticated Alpaca credentials were present. It recorded `exact_provider=unavailable` and produced no exact quote/trade results. Therefore bid/ask validation remains incomplete.

## Paper recommendation

- Retain Scalp Follow v2 behind a paper-only flag.
- Use Edge as a strict recent-BUY authorization gate for the next test.
- Carry 30 minutes as the primary challenger and 60 minutes as the higher-activity challenger.
- Keep Pulse's atomic frozen stop; do not enable the tested expiry reduction rule.
- Run an independent month and SPY/QQQ quote-sequenced validation before integrating into live execution.
